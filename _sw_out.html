The Old Man's Garden

Every morning, an old man works in his garden. He has a small house and a big garden. The garden is full of flowers, and there is one tree near the wall.

每天早上，一位老人在他的花园里干活。他有一间小房子和一个大花园。花园里开满了花，墙边有一棵树。

I walk past his house on my way to work. One day I stop and say, “Your garden is beautiful.”

我上班路上会经过他家。有一天我停下来，说：“你的花园真好看。”

He looks up and smiles. “Thank you. I work here every day, ten minutes in the morning and ten minutes in the evening.”

他抬起头笑了。“谢谢。我每天都来，早上十分钟，晚上十分钟。”

“Only twenty minutes?” I ask.

“只要二十分钟？”我问。

“Yes,” he says. “Many years ago I worked for hours, and I was always tired. Then I learned this: a little work every day is better than a lot of work in one day.”

“对，”他说，“很多年前我一干就是好几个小时，总是很累。后来我明白了一件事：每天做一点，比一天做很多更好。”

I think about his words for a long time. Now I read ten pages every night before I sleep. It is not much. But after one year, it is a lot.

我把他的话想了很久。现在每晚睡前我读十页书。不算多。但一年下来就很多了。

The old man still works in his garden. The flowers are always there. He does not hurry, and he does not stop.

老人如今还在花园里干活。花一直在那里。他不着急，也从不停止。

“Slow and steady wins the race.”稳扎稳打者胜
